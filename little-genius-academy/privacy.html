Privacy

Plain-language summary. Last updated:

This is a simple draft written to be honest and easy to read. It is not legal advice. Before a public launch, the owner should have it reviewed for the rules that apply where the families live (for example children's privacy laws such as COPPA and GDPR).

Who this is for

Little Genius Academy is made for children aged 2–6, but it is used and managed by a parent or guardian. Only parents create accounts.

What we collect

  • Parent: name, email address and password (the password is handled by our login service and is never visible to us).
  • Child profile: only a nickname, a cartoon avatar and a learning level. We do not ask for a child's photo, email, date of birth, address or any real-world contact details. Please use a nickname, not a full name.
  • Learning activity: which lessons and games were played, scores, badges earned and time spent, so parents can see progress and set screen-time limits.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no selling of data.
  • No chat or messaging between users.
  • No tracking of children across other websites.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, protected by access rules so a parent can only see their own children. Pages are delivered through our hosting provider.
