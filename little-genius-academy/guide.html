Parents' Guide

Quick answers about how Little Genius Academy works.

How do I start?

Create a parent account, log in, then add a child profile (a nickname and a cartoon avatar). Your child never needs an email or a password. You choose the child from the Parent area and they can start learning.

What can my child do here?

Learn letters, numbers, colors, shapes and animals; play games (matching, counting, memory, color sorting, shape building, tracing and puzzles); read picture stories and rhymes; and take short quizzes. Everything is made for ages 2 to 6.

Can I see my child's progress?

Yes. The Progress and Reports pages in the Parent area show lessons finished, game scores, badges and time spent. Progress is saved in your account, not only on one phone.

Can I limit screen time?

Yes. Each child has a daily time limit that you can set in the Parent area. When the time is up, your child sees a friendly "time's up" page.

Is there advertising or chat?

No advertising and no chat between users. Your child cannot contact anyone from the app.

The sound or voice does not work. What can I do?

Voices come from your phone or browser, so they can sound different on different devices. Check that the phone is not on silent, tap the 🔊 button, and try again. Newer versions of Chrome, Edge and Safari usually have the clearest voices.
