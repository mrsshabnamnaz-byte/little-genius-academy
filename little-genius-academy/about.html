About Little Genius Academy

Little Genius Academy is a playful learning world for children aged 2–6. Parents create the account and manage each child's profile, so children never need an email or password.

Learn through play

Short, gentle activities in letters, numbers, colors, shapes and animals.

Safe by design

No ads, no public profiles and no chat. Parents stay in control.

Grows with your child

Three learning levels, from Tiny Learners to Genius Explorers.
